Why You should Enroll Now!


Welcome to OB Montessori Center (OBMC) Fairview, where we empower students from preschool to senior high school through a progressive, Montessori-rooted education that nurtures independence, critical thinking, and real-world readiness right here in Quezon City.

• Casa Program (Ages 3 to 5 | Pre-K to Kindergarten): Cultivates early independence, personality development, and a lifelong love of discovery through sensory exploration and hands-on Montessori materials.
• Primary Grade School (Ages 6 to 8 | Grades 1 to 3): Bridges early childhood and structured academics using tactile, geometric learning tools to build strong foundations in literacy and numeracy.
• Intermediate Grade School (Ages to 12 | Grades 4 to 6): Fosters collaboration and "learning to live together" through expanded studies in science and invention, diverse literature, and practical life skills like culinary arts.
• Junior High School (Ages 13 to 16 | Grades 7 to 10): Focuses on self-development and "learning to be" with design-based disciplines, leadership opportunities, and deeper scientific inquiry.
• Senior High School (Ages 17 to 18 | Grades 11 to 12): Prepares young adults for university and career success in a focused academic setting equipped with modern digital tools for specialized study.
