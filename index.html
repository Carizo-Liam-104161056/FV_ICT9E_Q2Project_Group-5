What is OB Montessori?


OB Montessori Center (Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center) is a private school with five campuses that utilize the montessori method to teach your child how to think independently for themselves. We want to raise your child for the betterment of their lives or for the future, we teach hands-on activities, we allow them to explore both the traditional and innovative side of the world. In this environment, we unsure your child will grow in a vibrant, helpful, and supportive community as they learn.
